Our Mission

Built in Africa. For Africa.

UmojaServ — from umoja, the Swahili word for unity. We bring small businesses and the digital world together. Quietly. Beautifully. On purpose.

We grew up watching our parents, our aunts, our neighbors run extraordinary businesses with their hands.

The tailor who knew every customer's measurements by memory. The salon owner who remembered every birthday. The mechanic who could diagnose an engine by sound alone.

Brilliance, everywhere. And almost none of it visible online.

We believe the next decade of African commerce belongs to the businesses that show up where their customers already are — on their phones. Not with bloated software built for Silicon Valley. With tools simple enough to use on the busiest day, powerful enough to change everything by the end of the month.

That's why we built UmojaServ. Not to teach business owners technology. To make technology quietly serve them.
